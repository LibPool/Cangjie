# ulid4cj

**Tag**: algorithm

## 简介

ULID (Universally Unique Lexicographically Sortable Identifier) for Cangjie: 48-bit millisecond timestamp + 80-bit randomness, Crockford Base32 text form, monotonic generator with injectable clock
